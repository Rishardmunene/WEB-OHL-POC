## What we will not do

- Invent a new slogan.
- Treat Preempt / Prevent / Secure as three business units or service cards.
- Read “Secure” as cybersecurity, shields, or padlocks.
- Make the Group headline “employment law experts” or “your in-house HR lawyers.”
- Stamp the three words on every entity card.

## Hierarchy after this pass

1. **Brand** — Ojijo Group
2. **Philosophy** — preempt. Prevent. Secure.
3. **Proposition** — anticipate risk, strengthen compliance and governance, protect people, processes and interests
4. **Expertise** — verified capabilities, each with a philosophy lens
5. **Entities** — specialist destinations
6. **Training** — verified programmes → Luma
